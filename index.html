About this project

Affordable Academic Books for Every Student

Student Book Exchange is a college mini-project that helps students buy, sell and exchange academic books at affordable prices. It encourages students to reuse useful books instead of leaving them unused.

The project brings buying, selling, searching and exchanging together in one beginner-friendly frontend website.
